Why this exists

Wikipedia is one of the best things humans have made. It runs on a couple of data centers in Virginia and Texas. Khan Academy, Stack Overflow, Project Gutenberg too. Most of what you'd want to read in your lifetime depends on a power grid you don't control.

Apocalypse is the simplest way to put 1.2 TB of human knowledge on a hard drive and have it work forever, with or without the internet. A working AI sits on top so you can ask questions instead of just searching.

"The marginal cost of completeness is near zero. Do the whole thing."

What it's built on

Apocalypse is a thin layer over a stack of incredible open-source projects. The real work was done by other people. I just glued it together and made it nice to use.

Kiwix
ZIM format and content library
openZIM
The ZIM files themselves
Mozilla llamafile
Local LLM inference
Meta Llama 3
The actual model
libzim
Python bindings for ZIM
pystray + Pillow
Cross-platform tray UI
PyInstaller
Self-contained app bundles
Wikipedia, Khan, SO, etc.
